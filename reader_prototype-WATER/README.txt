WATER READER — UNDERWATER LIFE + BURIED VOICE

This pass keeps the no-whistle underwater ambience and adds a deliberately buried spoken signal.

Sound layers:
- deep diffuse submerged bed
- distant low marine movement
- sparse aquatic-plant / hydrophone-like clicks and pops
- rare low bog / mud bubbles
- irregular water drops
- one spoken phrase, heavily muffled and routed through the Reader's FREQ/BAND search filter

The spoken phrase is intentionally not audible as a foreground voice. It sits inside the water texture and should become easier to recognize as the player searches the frequency/band controls.

The ambience references the character of underwater/hydrophone ambience on Pixabay, including continuous deep-water beds and underwater atmosphere. The reference page used during development:
https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/underwater/

Run phone/index.html in a browser or serve the folder locally. Press LISTEN.
